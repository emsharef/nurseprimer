<MathDrawer title="a weighted vote">
  Each word $w$ in a message has a weight $\theta_w$. The message's score is the sum of the weights of its words,

  $$
  s(x) = b + \sum_{w \in x} \theta_w ,
  $$

  and the machine says urgent when $s(x) > 0$.
</MathDrawer>
